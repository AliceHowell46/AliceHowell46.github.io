Privacy Policy for Drum Diary

Last updated: 09/09/2026

Overview

Drum Diary ("the App") is designed with your privacy as a priority. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Data Storage

All data you create in the App — including practice session logs, goals, notation entries, custom templates, tags, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, using your device's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to us
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service by the App itself
  • Will be deleted if you uninstall the App, unless you have enabled your device's own backup system (e.g., iCloud Backup), which is controlled by your device settings, not by us

Advertising

Drum Diary displays advertisements to free-tier users through Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may collect and process the following to deliver and measure ads:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier / IDFA)
  • General device information (device type, operating system)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

App Tracking Transparency: On iOS, we request your permission before using your device's advertising identifier for personalized ads, in compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You can decline this permission at any time; you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you (non-personalized).

You can review Google's own privacy practices at Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Purchasing the Pro upgrade removes all advertisements from the App.

In-App Purchases

Drum Diary offers an optional one-time "Pro" purchase. This purchase is processed entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit system. We do not receive, store, or process your payment information — this is handled exclusively by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

To verify your purchase, the App communicates with Apple's servers to confirm your entitlement status.

Third-Party Services

Drum Diary uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free-tier users, as described above. We do not use any other third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

Drum Diary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App's advertising features, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Your Rights

Data you create within the App is stored locally on your device and is entirely under your control — you can delete it at any time by clearing it within the App or by uninstalling the App entirely. For data collected by Google AdMob for advertising purposes, please refer to Google's privacy resources linked above for information on your choices and rights regarding that data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact
